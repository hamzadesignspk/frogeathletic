Frequently asked questions

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes — book a free trial session and try the facility, a class, and meet a coach before committing to a plan.

Is there a minimum contract period?

Monthly plans have no lock-in period. Quarterly and annual plans are billed upfront but can be paused for genuine reasons like travel.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes — memberships can be paused for up to 30 days a year for medical or travel reasons. Just let the front desk know in advance.

Do I need my own equipment?

No — all training equipment and mats are provided. Just bring proper training shoes and a water bottle.

Is personal training included in membership?

Performance and Elite plans include a set number of PT sessions each month. Foundation members can book sessions separately as a package.

Do you offer corporate memberships?

Yes — we offer corporate packages for teams of 5 or more. Message us on WhatsApp for a custom quote.

Is parking available?

Yes, dedicated member parking is available on-site.
